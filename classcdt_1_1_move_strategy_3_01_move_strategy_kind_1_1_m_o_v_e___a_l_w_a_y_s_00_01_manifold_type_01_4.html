template<typename ManifoldType>
auto cdt::MoveStrategy< MoveStrategyKind::MOVE_ALWAYS, ManifoldType >::operator() ( ManifoldType const & t_manifold) -> ManifoldType
inlinenodiscard

Execute a fresh run while continuing the owned random stream.

The input remains unchanged. Counters and checkpoint events are replaced only after the invocation completes. If an exception escapes, the owned random stream may already have advanced even though those published results remain unchanged.

Parameters
t_manifoldInitial canonical state for the run.
Returns
Canonical state after all configured passes complete.
Exceptions
std::filesystem::filesystem_errorif checkpoint output is enabled and persistence fails; also propagates failures from move generation and validation.
